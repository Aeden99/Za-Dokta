$$ I(z) \;=\; \int_\Gamma \Phi\,\varphi, \qquad \Phi \;=\; \prod_j P_j(x;z)^{c_j} \prod_i x_i^{\nu_i} $$

A Feynman integral in parametric form is an integral of this kind, a twisted period. The notes below look at it from several sides: how many independent integrals a family has, where they are singular, what functions they are, and what they look like at a singularity. The last question is the one being worked on. The notes are meant to be read together. A good place to start is The Bubble, Exactly, where everything is computed by hand, or the algebraic-structure note, which is the frame for the rest. The Reading Program says what was read and what is still open.
